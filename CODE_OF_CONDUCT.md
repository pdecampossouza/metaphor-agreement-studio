# Code of Conduct

Metaphor Agreement Studio is intended to support collaborative research. Participants in project spaces are expected to communicate professionally, discuss methods and code in good faith, and respect differences in disciplinary background and technical experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of private research data, and attempts to identify research participants or evaluators from protected data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.

If a disagreement concerns a scientific or statistical choice, focus discussion on assumptions, evidence, reproducible examples, and the consequences for interpretation rather than on individuals.
