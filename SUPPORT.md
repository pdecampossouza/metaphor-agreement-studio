# Support

## Questions and usage help

Open a GitHub issue with a concise description of what you are trying to do, your operating system, Python version, and the relevant application page. Screenshots are welcome when they do not contain confidential research material.

## Bug reports

Please include:

- Metaphor Agreement Studio version or commit;
- operating system and Python version;
- steps to reproduce;
- expected and observed behavior;
- traceback or request ID when available;
- a synthetic or redistributable example workbook when the bug depends on workbook structure.

Do **not** upload confidential doctoral data, participant information, unpublished lyrics, or restricted research workbooks to public issues.

## Methodological questions

Questions about why a statistic is used, its assumptions, or interpretation are welcome. The software intentionally distinguishes agreement, classification tendency, category association, and expert-benchmark evaluation.
